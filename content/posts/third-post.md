There is a profound difference between the absence of noise and the presence of silence. Noise distracts the mind, but silence allows it to breathe.

When we turn off our endless notifications, we hear the true rhythm of our own thoughts. The Renaissance thinkers knew the value of retreating to the study—a dedicated space for intellectual quietude.
