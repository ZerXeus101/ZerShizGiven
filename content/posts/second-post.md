Patience is not merely the ability to wait, but the ability to keep a good attitude while waiting. In a world of instant gratification, the slow and deliberate cultivation of virtue stands as a monument to true strength.

Through careful observation of nature, we learn that nothing of true value is built overnight. The grandest oaks were once small acorns that endured countless storms and droughts.
